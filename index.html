Welcome

A sanctuary with the backyard party feeling

Tucked on the west side of Aquidneck Island, Weathervane Cottages is a boutique retreat where comfort meets unplugged magic. Eight private cottages and a bungalow sit among gardens, lawns, and fire pits — with Narragansett Bay sunsets just beyond the trees.

We are two families — lifelong friends since the eighties — who believe hospitality should feel like coming home. Not a hotel. Not a resort. Just a place to recharge.

Aquidneck Island

Sunsets, harbors & the best of Rhode Island

We’re on the west side of Aquidneck Island — 4.5 miles from Newport, 7 miles from Bristol — with Narragansett Bay light in the evenings and small-town charm at the doorstep.

Narragansett Bay

West-facing evenings and water light that makes every arrival feel like a deep breath.

Newport — 4.5 miles

Mansions, harbor walks, and world-class sailing culture just a short drive south.

Bristol — 7 miles

Historic downtown, waterfront strolls, and one of New England’s favorite Fourth of July traditions.
