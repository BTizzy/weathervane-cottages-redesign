Our Story

Ninety years. One parcel of land. Two families.

From 1930s Sunset Cabins to Twin Lanterns RV Park to the Weathervane you see today—this ground has always been about gathering, resting, and finding your direction again.

Lifelong friends Eric & Cris Offenberg and Kevin & Krissy Coristine—Aquidneck Islanders, six kids between them—built this together. Family-owned hospitality with 30+ years of combined experience.

The Area

Aquidneck Island at your doorstep

West-facing sunsets over Narragansett Bay. Newport 4.5 miles south. Bristol 7 miles north. Serenity and exploration in perfect balance.

Narragansett Bay

Watch the sun drop over the water from your own fire pit—nightly, and never the same twice.

Newport

Mansions, the Cliff Walk, harbor sailing, and world-class dining—fifteen minutes from your cottage door.

Portsmouth & Beyond

Farms, vineyards, quiet beaches, and the East Bay Bike Path. Bristol’s Independence Day parade is a Rhode Island institution.

Questions

Good to know

How do I book a stay?

Book direct on our booking page, call (401) 366-8010, or email stay@weathervanecottages.com. Real humans, same-day replies.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Need flexibility? Reach out—we’ll do our best.

Can we host a wedding or reunion?

Absolutely. Full-property buyouts sleep 28 overnight and welcome up to 32 for day events. See Gatherings for details.

Where exactly are you located?

1172 West Main Road, Portsmouth, RI 02871—west side of Aquidneck Island, 4.5 miles from Newport, 7 miles from Bristol.
