Where the bay meets a quieter pace

A coastal sanctuary on Aquidneck Island.

Weathervane Cottages sits on the west side of Aquidneck Island, surrounded by cedar trees, stone walls, and hydrangea gardens. Eight classic New England cottages and four luxurious glamping tents offer a rare blend of privacy and community — perfect for couples, families, and intimate gatherings.

Whether you're escaping for a romantic weekend, gathering family, or hosting an intimate wedding, this is a place to slow down, reconnect, and remember what matters.

Common questions

Everything you need to know

How far is Weathervane Cottages from Newport?
Just 4.5 miles from downtown Newport and 7 miles from Bristol. You're perfectly positioned to enjoy both towns while staying in a much quieter setting.
Do you offer direct booking?
Yes. We strongly prefer direct reservations. You'll avoid third-party fees and get the best rates and availability — plus our team can personalize your stay.
Are the glamping tents heated and air-conditioned?
The tents have excellent insulation and ceiling fans. In cooler months we provide portable heaters. All cottages and the Bungalow have full AC and heat.
Is the property suitable for weddings?
Absolutely. We host intimate ceremonies and receptions. The lawn, courtyard, and fire pit area create beautiful backdrops. We accommodate up to 32 guests overnight.
What is the history of the property?
The land dates to the 1930s as Sunset Cabins, then became Twin Lanterns RV Park. Weathervane Cottages opened in 2024 by owners Eric & Cris Offenberg and Kevin & Krissy Coristine.
Are pets allowed?
Select cottages are pet-friendly. Please ask when booking. A small pet fee may apply.
Our story

From RV park to coastal sanctuary.

What was once Twin Lanterns RV Park is now Weathervane Cottages — a labor of love by longtime friends Eric & Cris Offenberg and Kevin & Krissy Coristine.

The name came from seeing weathervanes on beautiful New England homes during a research trip from Rhode Island through Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont, and New York. It felt like the perfect symbol for finding your direction again.

We opened the first four cottages in August 2024. The full property came online in early 2025. Today it is a place where people come to slow down, reconnect, and remember what matters.

— Eric Offenberg, Owner
