Newport County

Aquidneck Island at your doorstep

West-facing sunsets over Narragansett Bay. Newport 4.5 miles south. Bristol 7 miles north. Discover the perfect balance between serenity and exploration.

Location

Portsmouth, western shore

Weathervane sits at 1172 West Main Road on the west side of Aquidneck Island—tucked enough for peace, connected enough for adventure.

Wake to birdsong and garden light. Spend the day in Newport’s harbor or on a Bristol café patio. Return for fire pits and a bay sunset that stops conversation mid-sentence. That is the rhythm here.

  • 7 mi Bristol
  • 25 mi Providence
  • 70 mi Boston

Nearby

Things to see & do

A curated shortlist of Aquidneck and East Bay favorites—enough to fill a weekend or a week.

On your doorstep

Narragansett Bay Sunsets

The western shore gift. Watch from your fire pit, deck, or garden—no reservation required.

4.5 miles

Newport Mansions

The Breakers, Marble House, Rosecliff—Gilded Age splendor along Bellevue Avenue.

4.5 miles

Cliff Walk

3.5 miles of oceanfront path between mansion lawns and Atlantic surf. Wear good shoes.

Aquidneck Island

Farms & Vineyards

Farm stands, tasting rooms, and fields that feed the island’s tables. Ask us for current favorites.

Newport Harbor

Sailing & the Waterfront

Harbor cruises, sailing lessons, Bowen’s and Bannister’s Wharf—seafood with a view.

Island-wide

Beaches

First Beach, Second Beach, Sachuest—Atlantic swims and sandbar walks within easy reach.

7 miles

Downtown Bristol

America’s oldest Fourth of July parade, Thames Street shops, waterfront walks, and great coffee.

East Bay

East Bay Bike Path

Fourteen scenic miles from Bristol to Providence—rent bikes and make a day of it.

Newport

Dining & Nightlife

From clam shacks to tasting menus—Newport County punches well above its weight at the table.

Farther afield

Day trips

When you want a fuller day out—still back in time for the fire pit.

01

Providence

~45 minutes

RISD Museum, WaterFire (seasonal), Federal Hill dining, and a capital city with serious creative energy.

02

Block Island

Ferry from Point Judith or Newport (seasonal)

Bluffs, bikes, and beaches on Rhode Island’s beloved offshore escape.

03

Little Compton & Tiverton

~30–40 minutes

Quiet stone walls, farm stands, Sakonnet vineyards, and some of the state’s most beautiful back roads.

04

Cape Cod (west end)

~90 minutes

Cross the Bourne Bridge for a Falmouth or Woods Hole afternoon—then home to quieter shores.
