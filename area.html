The Area

The best of Aquidneck Island.

On the quieter west side of the island — 4.5 miles from Newport, 7 miles from Bristol. Beaches, farms, vineyards, sailing, and easy day trips up and down the New England coast.

Where you'll be

Better than central.

Located on the western side of Aquidneck Island, Weathervane gives you the best of both worlds: peaceful, residential surroundings with sunset views over Narragansett Bay — and a 15-minute drive to the bustle of Newport, the Cliff Walk, and the famous mansions.

You're 7 miles from Bristol (home of the oldest 4th of July parade in the country), 12 minutes to Sachuest Beach, and an easy day trip to Providence, Mystic CT, Cape Cod, or Boston.

  • 4.5 mi to Newport waterfront
  • 7 mi to Bristol historic district
  • 1.5 mi to Glen Farm (event venue)
  • 30 mi to Providence

Nearby favorites

From iconic cliff walks to pick-your-own farms — all a short drive from your cottage.

Newport

Cliff Walk

A 3.5-mile scenic walk along the eastern shore of Newport, with the ocean on one side and Gilded Age mansions on the other.

~ 15 min · 4.5 mi
Beaches

Sachuest & Second Beach

Rhode Island's most beloved beaches — soft sand, Atlantic surf, and food shacks within walking distance.

~ 12 min · 5 mi
Middletown

Sweet Berry Farm

Pick-your-own berries in season, outdoor lunch, fresh produce, and a great farm store. Family favorite.

~ 10 min · 4 mi
Portsmouth

Glen Farm

Historic event venue on 80 acres — home of the Portsmouth Polocrosse Club and a stunning wedding venue.

~ 5 min · 1.5 mi
Middletown

Newport Vineyards

Rhode Island's largest vineyard. Tastings, tours, and on-site restaurant. Live music on weekends.

~ 10 min · 4 mi
Bristol

Bristol Waterfront

Charming seaside town, home of the oldest 4th of July parade in America. Indie shops, restaurants, and harbor walks.

~ 12 min · 7 mi
Portsmouth

Clements' Marketplace

A local grocery shop with everything you need for a cozy night in — wine, fresh pasta, prepared foods, and a great bakery.

~ 5 min · 1.5 mi

Sailing & Harbor Cruises

Newport is the sailing capital of the world. Day sails, sunset cruises, and America's Cup history tours.

~ 15 min · 4.5 mi
Portsmouth

Newport Polo

Saturday matches all summer at Glen Farm. Bring a picnic, watch the game, and enjoy an authentic New England tradition.

~ 5 min · 1.5 mi
