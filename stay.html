Cottages 1–8 + Bungalow

The Cottages

Eight studio-style cottages and one two-bedroom Bungalow, each with a queen bed, full private bathroom, mini-fridge, microwave, Keurig, AC, and Wi-Fi. Cottage interiors feature two-tone wood-paneled ceilings, comfy seating, and thoughtful lighting. Outside, every cottage has its own private garden or deck with Adirondack chairs and a fire pit.

  • Queen bed · premium linens · extra pillows
  • Full private bath with zero-threshold shower
  • Mini-fridge, microwave, Keurig coffee
  • Split-unit AC & heat
  • Private garden (Cottages 1–4) or deck (Cottages 5–8)
  • Adirondack chairs, fire pit, soft lighting

Safari Tents

The Glamping Tents

Four luxury 440 sq ft safari-style canvas tents with queen beds, mini-fridges, and covered porches with outdoor furniture. The Bath House — shared with all four tents — has two full showers with hot water, towels, a hair dryer, and a clothes steamer.

  • Queen bed · premium linens
  • Mini-fridge & water jug
  • Covered porch with seating
  • Ceiling fans · portable heaters in cooler months
  • Shared Bath House (hot showers, towels, hair dryer)
  • Access to fire pit, lounge, BBQ, gardens

The Bungalow

Our two-bedroom Bungalow with a full kitchen, spacious living area, and the property's signature stone chimney. It's the warmest spot on the property in winter and the most spacious for families or anyone who wants room to spread out.

  • Full kitchen · dining area
  • Stone fireplace · full bath
  • Private garden · outdoor seating
  • AC & heat · Wi-Fi
