Stay with us

Experience the perfect blend of charm and modern convenience. Nestled amidst the serene landscapes of Aquidneck Island, each accommodation is a tranquil retreat where you can relax, recline, and recharge.

From plush bedding and a comfy seating area to mini-fridge, microwave, and Keurig with a modern bathroom—every detail has been carefully curated for your comfort. Step outside to trees, flowers, and grass. Private gardens or decks. Soft lighting. Fire pit flames. Adirondack chairs. The bay beyond.

Outdoor Privacy

Your own patch of sky

Step outside and immerse yourself in trees, flowers, and grass. Nature surrounds you—without sacrificing solitude.

Cottages 1–4, the tent sites, and the Bungalow each boast a spacious garden. Cottages 5–8 have roomy private decks. Every unit includes soft lighting, a fire pit, Adirondack chairs, and a small table—the perfect setting for alfresco dining, morning coffee, or unwinding with a book as the sun sets over the bay.
