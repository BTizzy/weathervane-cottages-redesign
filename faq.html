Help

Questions, answered

Straight answers from the people who built this place. Still curious? Call us—(401) 366-8010.

Booking

How do I book a stay at Weathervane Cottages?

Book direct through our booking page, call (401) 366-8010, or email stay@weathervanecottages.com. Real humans, same-day replies. No call centers.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request—reach out ahead of your stay and we will do our best.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms vary by season and booking type. We confirm the policy clearly at the time of reservation. Questions before you book? Call or email—happy to walk through it.

Property

Are pets allowed?

Select units may accommodate well-behaved pets with prior approval and a pet fee. Please contact us before booking so we can confirm the right fit for your dates.

Is parking available on site?

Yes. Complimentary on-site parking is available for all guests.

What amenities are in the studio cottages?

Each studio cottage includes a queen bed, full private bath with zero-threshold shower, mini-fridge, microwave, Keurig, split-unit AC/heat, Wi-Fi, Adirondack chairs, and a private fire pit. Cottages 1–4 have private gardens; cottages 5–8 have private decks.

Do the glamping tents have air conditioning and heat?

Glamping tents include a ceiling fan and are designed for seasonal comfort. They do not have full AC/heat like the cottages. The shared Bath House—steps away—offers two hot showers, towels, a hair dryer, and a clothes steamer.

Are linens and towels provided?

Yes. Quality linens and towels are provided in the cottages and the Bungalow. Glamping guests receive linens, with towel service at the shared Bath House.

Events

Can we host a wedding or large group?

Absolutely—gatherings are encouraged. Full-property buyouts sleep 28 overnight and welcome up to 32 day-event guests. We provide tables, chairs, tent, coolers, grill, serving ware, and more. You choose the offsite catering; we help make planning seamless. See Gatherings or reach out to start planning.

Do you host corporate retreats?

Yes. The property works well for small team retreats and offsites—private units for rest, flexible outdoor spaces for sessions and shared meals. Call us to talk through your group size and goals.

About

Where is Weathervane Cottages located?

1172 West Main Road, Portsmouth, Rhode Island 02871—west side of Aquidneck Island, with sunset views over Narragansett Bay. 4.5 miles from Newport, 7 miles from Bristol.

What is the history of the property?

This land began as Sunset Cabins in the 1930s, then Twin Lanterns RV Park from the 1980s through 2021—generations of family memories inside the stone walls. In late 2021, Eric and Cris Offenberg partnered with lifelong friends Kevin and Krissy Coristine to reimagine the property. After a research trip across New England, weathervanes on local homes became the namesake: a symbol for finding your direction again. First cottages opened August 2024; the full property in early 2025. Read more on Our Story.
