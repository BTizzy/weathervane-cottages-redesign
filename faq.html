Booking & Stay

Do you offer direct booking?
Yes. We strongly prefer direct reservations. You avoid third-party fees, get the best rates, and can customize your stay with us directly. Call (401) 366-8010 or use our booking form.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 3:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. If you need to arrive outside that window, please contact us in advance.
Are pets allowed?
Select cottages are pet-friendly. Please ask when booking. A small pet fee may apply.
Is parking available?
Yes — plenty of free on-site parking for all guests, including groups and event bookings.

The Property

How many guests can stay at Weathervane Cottages?
We sleep up to 28 guests across 8 studio cottages, 1 two-bedroom Bungalow, and 4 safari-style glamping tents. The property can host up to 32 for day events.
What is the difference between the cottages and the glamping tents?
Cottages are studio-style with a queen bed, full private bathroom, mini-fridge, microwave, AC, and Wi-Fi. The 2-bedroom Bungalow has a full kitchen. Glamping tents are 440 sq ft safari-style canvas with a queen bed, mini-fridge, covered porch, and shared access to a Bath House with hot showers, towels, and a hair dryer.
Is there air conditioning and heat in the tents?
The tents have ceiling fans and excellent insulation. In cooler months we provide portable heaters. All cottages and the Bungalow have full split-unit AC and heat.
Do you supply linens, towels, and toiletries?
Yes. All cottages, the Bungalow, and the glamping tents come with premium linens, pillows, and towels. The Bath House (for glamping guests) provides towels, a hair dryer, and a clothes steamer.

Events & Groups

Can I book a wedding or group event at Weathervane?
Yes. We host intimate weddings, micro-weddings, bachelorette weekends, family reunions, and corporate retreats. The entire property can be reserved, with multiple event lawns (The Lawn, The Woods, The Courtyard, The Patio), fire pits, lounge, and catering-ready amenities for up to 32 guests.
Can I host a corporate retreat or team offsite?
Absolutely. Our capacity up to 28 overnight, multiple event spaces, fast Wi-Fi, and the property's quiet setting make Weathervane a strong fit for small leadership retreats, team offsites, and strategy days.

About Us

Where is Weathervane Cottages located?
We are at 1172 West Main Road, Portsmouth, Rhode Island 02871, on the west side of Aquidneck Island with sunset views over Narragansett Bay. We are 4.5 miles from downtown Newport and 7 miles from Bristol.
What is the history of Weathervane Cottages?
The property dates back to the 1930s as Sunset Cabins, later became Twin Lanterns RV Park, and was reinvented as Weathervane Cottages in 2024 by owners Eric & Cris Offenberg and Kevin & Krissy Coristine.
