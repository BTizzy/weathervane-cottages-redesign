Booking & Stay

Book direct through our site or reach us at (401) 366-8010 or stay@weathervanecottages.com. Direct booking means personal service and the best sense of which cottage fits your group.

Each cottage includes a queen bed, full private bath, mini-fridge, microwave, Keurig, air conditioning, and Wi-Fi. Linens and towels are provided. The gardens, fire pits, and gathering spaces are open for you to enjoy.

We’ll confirm exact times with your reservation. If you need an early arrival or late departure, just ask — we’ll do our best to accommodate when the schedule allows.

The Property

We have eight private cottages, plus a bungalow and glamping options. Together with our gathering spaces, the property can host up to 28 overnight guests.

Yes. Wi-Fi is available so you can stay connected when you need to — and unplug when you don’t. Most guests find the fire pits more compelling than their inboxes.

We’re on the west side of Aquidneck Island with sunset views toward Narragansett Bay. Ask us for the best nearby spots to put your feet in the water or catch the evening light.

Events & Groups

Yes. We host intimate gatherings for up to 32 guests across four outdoor spaces — Courtyard, Lawn, Woods, and Patio — with overnight capacity for 28. Reach out and we’ll walk you through options.

Full-property and multi-cottage bookings are some of our favorite weekends. Contact us with your dates and group size and we’ll see what’s possible.

Fire pits are part of the Weathervane magic. We’ll share guidelines when you arrive so every evening feels safe, warm, and memorable.
