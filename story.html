Our Story

Rooted in friendship.
Grown with care.

Two families, lifelong friends since the 1980s, bringing decades of hospitality experience to a property with deep Aquidneck Island roots.

“We wanted a place that feels like the best backyard party you’ve ever been to — warm, unfussy, and full of heart. That’s the Weathervane we’re building.”

— Eric Offenberg

Through the Years

A property with a past — and a future

  1. 1930s

    Sunset Cabins

    The property’s story begins as Sunset Cabins — a roadside retreat on the west side of Aquidneck Island, welcoming travelers with simple island hospitality.

  2. 1980s

    A lifelong friendship

    Eric & Cris Offenberg and Kevin & Krissy Coristine become lifelong friends — a bond that will, decades later, shape the next chapter of this land.

  3. 2021

    A new chapter begins

    The two families step into ownership — bringing more than thirty years of combined experience in real estate, vacation rentals, and hosting to Weathervane.

  4. 2022

    Restoring the welcome

    Careful improvements begin — cottages refreshed, grounds tended, and the spirit of unplugged hospitality put at the center of every decision.

  5. 2023

    Gatherings take root

    The outdoor spaces come into their own — Courtyard, Lawn, Woods, and Patio — welcoming reunions, celebrations, and fire-lit evenings.

  6. 2024

    Comfort, refined

    Interiors, amenities, and the guest experience continue to deepen — queen beds, private baths, and the little details that make a stay feel like home.

  7. 2025

    Still the backyard feeling

    Today the property includes eight cottages, a bungalow, glamping, and four gathering spaces — still family-owned, still rooted in friendship, still inviting you to relax, recline, and recharge.
