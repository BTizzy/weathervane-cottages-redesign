Heritage

Ninety years. One parcel of land.

This ground has always been about gathering, resting, and finding your direction again. Two families simply carried the story forward.

The weathervanes seen on every beautiful New England home along the way became our namesake—the perfect symbol for finding your direction again.

The Arc

A living piece of Aquidneck history

  1. 1930s

    Sunset Cabins

    An iconic roadside motor lodge takes root on the western shore. Travelers stop for rest, sunsets, and the simple magic of a night by the bay.

  2. 1980s–2021

    Twin Lanterns RV Park

    Generations of family memories accumulate inside the stone walls. Summer returns, year after year. The land keeps its vocation: hospitality.

  3. 2021

    A conversation between friends

    Eric and Cris Offenberg notice the property. They contact longtime friends Kevin and Krissy Coristine to discuss a partnership. Lifelong Aquidneck Islanders. Lifelong friends since the ’80s. Six kids between them in the local community.

  4. 2022

    The research road trip

    Rhode Island to Massachusetts to New Hampshire to Vermont to New York—visiting tiny homes, cabin parks, glamping sites, and motels. Along the route, weathervanes mounted on New England homes catch their eye again and again. The name clicks. The vision sharpens.

  5. 2023

    Plans take shape

    Business plan. Marketing concept. Permitting. Financing. The slow, deliberate work of building something meant to last—another 90 years, if they get it right.

  6. 2024

    First cottages open

    August 2024: the first four cottages welcome guests. Relax. Recline. Recharge.—the philosophy on the sign—comes to life.

  7. 2025

    Full property complete

    Early 2025: the remaining four cottages, four glamping tents, and the Bungalow open. Thirteen units. One family-owned hospitality business with 30+ years combined experience in real estate, vacation rentals, and hosting.

The Families

Two families. Lifelong friends.

Not a management company. Not a chain. People who live here, raise kids here, and care deeply about how you feel when you stay.

Eric & Cris Offenberg

Real estate · Vacation rentals · Design vision

The eye for property, the instinct for what makes a space feel like sanctuary. Cris and Eric bring decades of real estate and rental experience—and a clear design point of view—to every cottage detail.

Kevin & Krissy Coristine

Hospitality · Hosting · The backyard party feeling

The hosts’ hosts. Kevin and Krissy know how to make people feel instantly at ease—the unforced warmth of a great backyard party, elevated into a stay you’ll remember.

Relax. Recline. Recharge.

When guests enter Weathervane, magic happens. They set aside their duties, responsibilities, and worries. They relax upon comfy beds in the sanctuary of their own tiny home. They recline peacefully to sleep the night away with unplugged phones and screens, to be recharged for the day ahead.

— Our founding philosophy
